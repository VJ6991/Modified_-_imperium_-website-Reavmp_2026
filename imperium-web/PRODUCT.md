# PRODUCT.md — imperiumapp.com rebuild

## What Imperium is
Imperium Software Technologies (Dubai HQ, founded 2005; offices in Singapore, Chennai, Bengaluru) helps enterprises connect customer engagement, communications infrastructure and applied AI. It builds and deploys platforms (iConnect is its own; Inaipi and Listen IQ are platforms it deploys and supports — exact relationship pending owner sign-off) and provides consulting, integration, deployment, modernization and 24×7 managed support. Heritage: telephony, CTI and Avaya integration since 2005.

## Who the site is for
- Primary: heads of customer service / CX, COOs, CIOs and IT managers at mid-to-large organisations in the GCC, Africa and South Asia — banking, government, telecom, healthcare, hospitality, insurance, retail and real estate.
- Secondary: evaluators (IT, procurement) checking integrations, deployment and security; partners.
- Scene: office desktop during the working day; often first touch is a phone tapping a LinkedIn link.

## What the site must do
1. Within one viewport, say what Imperium does and who it serves, and offer "Talk to an Expert".
2. Lead with buyer outcomes; back each with how it works; show proof only where proof is approved.
3. Route visitors by problem (Solutions), platform (Products) or sector (Industries).

## Content rules (from the 03 Nov 2026 content audit)
- No unattributed performance metrics. The legacy 40% / 30% / 2X / 100% figures and the iConnect deck's AHT/FCR ranges are NOT to be published.
- Do not imply partner technology is Imperium IP.
- Customer names/logos: only those already public on the live site or in already-public case studies, pending re-confirmation.
- Website chatbot (if added later) is text-only.
- Primary CTA everywhere: "Talk to an Expert". Contextual: "Request a demo", "Discuss your requirements".
- Banned words: seamless, unlock, empower, revolutionize, next-gen, cutting-edge, best-in-class, synergy.
