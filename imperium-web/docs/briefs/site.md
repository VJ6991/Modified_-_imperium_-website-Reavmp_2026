# Surface brief — imperiumapp.com (all marketing routes)

Mode: Persuade (home, solutions, products, industries), Read (insights, case studies, legal).
Unresolved: product relationship wording (Inaipi, Listen IQ), proof approvals, logo SVG, photography.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a route map. Imperium's job is routing every customer conversation — voice, chat, WhatsApp, email, social — to one place where it gets handled. The page refuses the category default (navy gradient, glowing AI brain, headset stock photo, row of three icon cards).

OWN-WORLD: Transit-network wayfinding. Paper-white ground, cool mist bands, near-black ink. Lines are the component language: each capability pillar is a coloured line (Customer Experience = Imperium orange, Enterprise Communications = signal blue, AI & Automation = teal), stops are white station dots with ink rings, interchanges are ink-ringed capsules. One signage typeface family (Overpass, Highway-Gothic lineage) in heavy weights for headings, regular for text; Overpass Mono only for measurements, times and counts. Square-ish corners (4px), hairline rules, no drop-shadow cards.

STORY: Visitor sees channels converge on one desk, understands "Imperium connects every interaction", believes it through specifics (Avaya IP Office, named case studies, real interface), then picks their route: problem, platform or industry, ending at Talk to an Expert.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left-aligned headline at ~4.5rem over two lines, supporting copy and two actions directly under it. Beneath, a full-width network map: five channel lines enter from the left, converge on an interchange labelled "One agent desktop", and leave right toward stops (CRM record, Supervisor view, Reports). Conversation dots travel the lines (reduced-motion: static). On phones the map turns vertical.

FORM: Wayfinding system / transit diagram, position 1 on the ordered list. seed: none (unavailable) — impeccable CLI not installed; direction chosen manually.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: Conversation dots travelling the hero lines into the interchange; the same line-and-station grammar reused for pillars, industries (stations served by lines) and delivery steps.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
